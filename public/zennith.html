The challenge

Public clinics struggle with three linked problems: medication stock-outs, long queues and paper records.

Zennith tackles all three with role-based tools for doctors, nurses, pharmacists, receptionists and patients. It is built on React, TypeScript and Firebase, and is piloting at Hillbrow Community Health Centre.

My role

As group leader and backend developer, I designed the database and moved the app from mock data to live Firebase. I also built the nurse OCR (optical character recognition) feature and set up two-way SMS appointment reminders that patients can reply to.

Behind the scenes, I plan our sprints, keep the project logs and open every pitch.

Highlights

  • ✦A database schema with practitioner registration numbers and separate storage for login credentials
  • ✦Secure sign-in with two-factor authentication and role-based access
  • ✦SMS reminders where patients reply 1 or 2 to confirm, and reception is told straight away
  • ✦Sprint planning that covers POPIA (Protection of Personal Information Act) compliance and Zero Trust security

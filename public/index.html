About me

I'm a final-year BCom Information Systems student at the University of Johannesburg, with an LLB and a BA from Rhodes University behind me. I build full-stack products and do legal-tech contracting at Libryo.

My work sits at the point where regulation meets code: clinic systems built around POPIA (Protection of Personal Information Act), timestamped records for informal trade, and AI that reads legislation so compliance teams don't have to start from scratch.
